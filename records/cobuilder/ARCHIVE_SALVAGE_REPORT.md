## 1. Scope and method

**What was searched.** The Originator's Google Drive and OneDrive, for documents mentioning Omega42, Omega 42 or The Construct.

**What exists.** The Google Drive register of 19 November already counts at least 3,617 matching items. This pass worked through the earliest material first, back to October 2025, and then through each later era:

- the Theory-of-Everything papers of October 2025;

- the protocol essays of August and September 2026.

On OneDrive, the "Omega42" and "Construct" searches each returned more than 50 documents, mostly protocol builds and Construct versions.

**How the material was handled.** The originals were read only and left unchanged. Personal and biographical material, and anything about games of chance, is left out of this report, as the archive policy requires. Every arithmetic statement below was recomputed.

**What remains.** Thousands of items are still unread. This report is a first salvage, not an inventory.

## 6. What did not carry forward, and why

The archive's boldness is its strength, and the current Omega42 keeps that spirit on its front page. What it does not keep are statements presented as confirmed without a derivation or a source:

- the percentages of "convergence";
- the confirmed anomalies;
- the funding estimates;
- the readings of history, the economy and ancient monuments.

None of this material is deleted; it remains in the archive, unchanged. Any item can return through the Speculative Foundations program or the Scientific Appendix, labeled for what it is.
