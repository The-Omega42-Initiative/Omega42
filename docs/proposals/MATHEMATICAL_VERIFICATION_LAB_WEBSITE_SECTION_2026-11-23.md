# Proposed website section: Omega42 Mathematical Verification Lab

**Proposal only — 23 November 2026.** For Claude and the user to review after the website freeze. Do not deploy or change homepage/navigation without explicit approval.

## Suggested heading

**Omega42 Mathematical Verification Lab**

**Invitation:** *Mathematicians, number theorists, dynamical-systems researchers and mathematical physicists are invited to independently test the Omega42 mathematical collection. Our goal is rigorous scrutiny: reproduce proofs, challenge assumptions, seek counterexamples and help distinguish established theorems from conjectures or interpretations.*

## Suggested sections

1. **The mathematical collection** — an indexed, versioned map of definitions, theorem statements, proof dependencies, numerical checks and unresolved questions. Distinguish canonical Core/Companion text from research proposals and computational evidence.
2. **Reproduce a result** — link directly to an exact theorem statement, proof, executable verification script, expected output, supported Python version and Git commit. Provide a no-install standard-library path where possible.
3. **Try to break it** — publish explicit falsification tasks: find a counterexample to the shear orbit invariant; test arbitrary congruence depths; independently check norm-surjectivity; verify centralizer order; examine the cat-map Weyl orbit-sum algebra and its Gram matrix.
4. **Open mathematical questions** — tag each item as proved, conditionally proved, numerically checked, conjectural, or interpretation. Display known proof dependencies and limits.
5. **Submit an independent review** — invite GitHub issues or pull requests with theorem identifier, assumptions, smallest counterexample, reproducible commands, exact outputs and references. Credit reviewers only with consent; provide a clear correction and version-history policy.
6. **Mathematics versus interpretation** — state prominently that finite arithmetic, spectral coincidences and orbit-count identities do not establish physical or consciousness claims.

## Suggested primary calls to action

- **Browse the mathematical collection**
- **Run the verification tests**
- **Submit a counterexample or correction**
- **Review open proof obligations**

Each should link to a real, maintained resource before publishing; do not invent working URLs.

## Initial challenge cards

- **MF-43:** Independently prove or falsify the all-depth shear-orbit classification; test its reliance on pure Fibonacci norm-square completeness.
- **Centralizer:** Check that C=I+3A generates all primitive Fibonacci-orbit classes modulo 3^k.
- **Fourier filtration:** Verify that shear averaging retains precisely the prescribed cyclic characters and that the refinement bands are orthogonal.
- **MF-185:** Compute Weyl orbit-sum Gram matrices and algebra products; test whether the arithmetic parity-even label sector supports a genuine operator-algebra embedding.
- **Canonical parity:** Confirm that public claims and reproducible proof files correspond to the frozen Core/Companion specifications.

## Publication safeguards

No claim of formal peer review until an external review is actually completed. No claim of novel mathematical discovery without literature comparison. No promises of prize, institutional endorsement or journal acceptance. Do not expose private reviewer data. Maintain versioned errata and reproducible tests. Explicitly separate the independent verification invitation from promotion of physical or philosophical interpretations.
