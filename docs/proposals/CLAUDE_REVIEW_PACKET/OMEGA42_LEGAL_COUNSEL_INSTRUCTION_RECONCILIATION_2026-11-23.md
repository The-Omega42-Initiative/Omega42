## Founder's controlling objective

Establish an institution that can maintain the Omega42 identity, open public record, infrastructure, research and lawful commercial services **without acquiring exclusive ownership or control of the eligible intellectual commons**. The Originator's founding and authorship roles must be acknowledged without automatic assignment of the Originator's pre-existing work or other contributors' work.

The client has proposed **a stewardship NPC first**, with an NPC-owned operating (Pty) Ltd only when commercially warranted, and a separate Certification Body only when operationally warranted. **This is a preferred design for legal comparison, not authority to incorporate.** Counsel must compare an NPC, a customised (Pty) Ltd, and the staged two-entity design, including costs, governance, tax, statutory duties, liability and cross-border implications.

## Decisions and safeguards counsel must settle before incorporation

1. **Structure and control.** Confirm whether an NPC without members is legally workable for the seven-person Committee/five-director board design, including initial incorporators, appointment/removal, quorums, independent directors, board duties and Originator's reserved consent. Explain what can be entrenched in an MOI and what requires binding contracts or separate institutions.
2. **Non-enclosure definition.** Define *covered material* by documented acceptance, rights clearance, applicable licence and publication; exclude unrelated independently developed work. Distinguish unprotectable mathematical ideas from copyrightable expression, patentable inventions, confidential know-how, trademarks and databases. A declaration alone cannot extinguish a third party's rights.
3. **Company patent promise.** Draft enforceable no-filing, no-acquisition, no-assertion, no-exclusive-licensing and anti-circumvention provisions, with treatment of rights acquired involuntarily, successor transactions and lawful remedies. Test whether absolute wording is legally feasible and what substitute is strongest.
4. **Contributor patent undertaking.** Make participation and the scope of commitment explicit, signed by the actual rights holder or authorised agent; address employers, universities, joint owners, controlled entities, improvements, territorial coverage, duration, assignments and enforceability by beneficiaries. Never claim that non-signatories are bound.
5. **Open licensing and publication.** Establish licence-by-material categories, provenance and rights-clearance checks, permission to publish, exact public timestamps and archives, third-party content controls, withdrawal/correction rules and review of disclosure before potential patent rights are lost. Copyright licences and patent non-assertion are not interchangeable.
6. **Brand and independence.** Define permitted descriptive and referential trademark use, official endorsement, certification independence and the conditions for transfer of marks. No identity policy may be used to suppress lawful criticism or independent use of openly licensed work.
7. **Continuity.** Specify succession, incapacitation, resignation, governance deadlock, insolvency, backup archives, domains and record custody. Dissolution provisions must respect creditors and mandatory NPC asset-distribution rules; an MOI cannot simply guarantee that all assets or marks will be released to the public.
8. **Operational and tax compliance.** Obtain specialist advice on CIPC filings and beneficial ownership, NPC reporting, SARS income tax/PBO/section 18A (none automatic), VAT, PAYE/UIF, POPIA/PAIA, cross-border privacy and foreign IP rules. Verify current thresholds and filing dates directly before advising or filing.
9. **AI contributions.** Review authorship, ownership, inventorship, provider terms, provenance, confidentiality and permissions jurisdiction by jurisdiction. Do not assume AI output has identical IP treatment worldwide.
10. **Conflict mapping.** Provide a written redline table showing every necessary change to `ENTITY.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, `PATENT_PLEDGE.md`, `PRIOR_ART.md`, `LICENSES.md`, `TRADEMARK.md`, `COMMERCIAL.md` and `CERTIFICATION.md`. No repository policy changes become effective merely by drafting this memo.
