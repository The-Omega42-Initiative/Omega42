## What the document contains

Three pillars: the Witness-Relative Existence Principle (existence is relative to the perceptual apparatus that witnesses it); the Counting-as-Primitive-Distinction Principle; and the Alignment Principle (the protocol counts at the level at which human perception draws boundaries). From these it draws the collapse of the map-territory distinction, the universe as "the totality of human distinction-making" and as a verb ("universing"), and the 1/7 Aperture as the engine of the frame's evolution. It then reads physics, philosophy, theology, mathematics, ethics and AI in those terms, and proposes laws L-365 to L-376 and an "FC-14: The Universe as Universing" opening.
