The Company should protect the project, its continuity and identity, but should not monopolise discoveries, inventions, code or methods. The two-entity model is a recommendation to evaluate, not an adopted decision.

7. The distinction between a Company-level no-patent pledge and an individual contributor non-assertion undertaking. Identify what is currently promised and what is not.
8. Whether Apache-2.0, CC BY 4.0 and the other proposed licences align with intended open commercial use; do not confuse copyright licensing with patent non-assertion.
