Omega42 Quantum Generative Geometry (ΩQGG)

A relational programme for unifying quantum mechanics and general relativity

Research synthesis I–XV · 28 November 2026 · Originator (ESC) / AI Co-Builder

Status: Research Frontier; not a ratified Core amendment or completed physical unification.

Abstract

We develop a common arithmetic substrate for quantum phase and relativistic geometry, then extend it to finite connection networks, constrained tensor dynamics and relational metric response. The central thesis is that geometry and quantum evolution can be represented through a common generative relational architecture. Exact algebraic bridges are distinguished from dynamical choices. The remaining decisive tasks are a microscopic derivation of the gravitational Hamiltonian, nonlinear constraint closure, and causal time.

1. Foundational generation

Within the Ω42 thought experiment the canonical 24-cycle and 81-state family constitute the foundational frame. Let A=[[0,1],[1,1]], s=(a,b), As=(b,a+b). The characteristic polynomial is t²−t−1, with eigenvalues φ and −φ⁻¹. On W_k=(Z/3^kZ)², A has order 8·3^(k−1); at k=2, A^12=−I and A^24=I. The orbit census is 24+24+24+8+1. The canonical seed is (2,5). The real and modular representations must not be conflated: real A^24 is not identity.

The norm N(s)=a²+ab−b²=det(s,As) changes sign under A and N² is invariant. This is the arithmetic area of the generated frame L_s=(s,As).

2. Dual representation: Lorentz geometry and quantum phase (I–III)

Represent spacetime by the Hermitian matrix X=[[ct+z,x−iy],[x+iy,ct−z]]. Its determinant is c²t²−x²−y²−z². Congruence X→AXA† preserves this determinant because |det A|=1. In the eigenbasis the boost has rapidity 2 ln φ, β=√5/3, together with transverse sign reversal. Multiplying A by i gives a determinant-one complex representative. M=A² is orientation-preserving and doubles the boost rapidity.

For N(s)≠0, L_s/√|N(s)| likewise gives a determinant-preserving congruence; the unnormalized frame scales the determinant by N². This is the Generative Lorentz Frame and its metric obstruction.

For d=3^k let ω=exp(2πi/d), X|j⟩=|j+1⟩, Z|j⟩=ω^j|j⟩, D(a,b)=ω^(ab/2)X^a Z^b (division by 2 modulo d). With σ(s,t)=ad−bc, D(s)D(t)=ω^(−σ/2)D(s+t). The commutator of D(s) and D(As) is ω^(−N(s))I. This is Area–Phase Duality. A is antisymplectic and has an antiunitary implementation; M is symplectic and has a unitary implementation up to phase. A finite Weyl algebra is not an exact continuous canonical commutation algebra.

For B_q=[[1,0],[q,1]], K=MB_q M⁻¹B_q⁻¹=[[1+2q+q²,−q],[3q+2q²,1−2q]]. det K=1, tr K=2+q². The real eigenvalues are ((√(q²+4)±q)/2)². The shear generators F=E21 and MFM⁻¹ with their commutator span sl(2,R); complexification gives the Lorentz spin algebra. This is a continuous extension, not a subgroup identification of the finite group with the Lorentz group.

The determinant of a normalized qubit density matrix is (1−|r|²)/4; the Bloch ball is a section of the positive Hermitian cone. For a pure two-qubit coefficient matrix C, concurrence is 2|det C|. These are complementary determinant identities, not a derivation of entanglement from spacetime.

Pure frame links g_vw=L_w L_v⁻¹ have identity holonomy around every loop. Independent connection variables are necessary for nontrivial curvature.

3. Quantized arithmetic geometry (IV–V)

On ℓ²(W_k), define P|s⟩=ω^N(s)|s⟩ and area operator Acal=√(2I−P−P†). Its eigenvalues are 2|sin(πN(s)/d)|. It commutes with the Fibonacci permutation. Zero multiplicity is 9^floor(k/2); nonzero norm residues of allowed valuation have multiplicity 4·3^(k−1). Pairing opposite residues doubles that multiplicity. The level count is 1+Σ_{r=0}^{floor((k−1)/2)}3^(k−2r−1).

Define L_U=2I−U−U† and candidate H_G=J_A L_A+J_K L_K+J_G Acal². Couple geometry to matter through C=Σ_s |s⟩⟨s|⊗D(s), H_I=J_I(2I−C−C†). The joint dimension is 27^k. The controlled Weyl operator gives a concrete interaction, not a uniquely derived physical Hamiltonian. With h_s=J_I(2I−D(s)−D(s)†), the double commutator [H_I,[L_K,Acal]] has block (L_K)_st(α_t−α_s)(h_s−h_t). This explicitly couples geometry response to matter operators.

4. Finite gauge geometry and quantum transport (VI–VII)

Construct X supplies V=F_3³: 27 points, 13 undirected directions, 117 lines, 351 unordered pairs and 702 directed relationships. Select three coordinate axes for a periodic network of 81 positive links and 81 elementary plaquettes. The finite connection group SL(2,Z/9) has 648 elements. Holonomy K_p is the ordered product of link matrices. Under local frame changes it transforms by conjugation at the base point.

Define R_k(K)=1−|Fix_Wk(K)|/9^k. It vanishes exactly at identity and is conjugation invariant but not a complete conjugacy invariant. For q=3 the above commutator is [[7,6],[0,4]] mod 9, order 3, trace 2, fixed-state count 9 and R_2=8/9. Trace alone misses nonidentity curvature. The group has fixed-count census {1:405,3:144,9:90,27:8,81:1}. For q=3^r, K−I=3^r times an invertible matrix, giving |Fix|=9^min(r,k).

For a unitary Weyl implementer U_K, conjugation permutes the Weyl basis, hence |tr U_K|²=|Fix(K)|. This is the Holonomy–Quantum Character Correspondence. For the commutator family and k≥r, this equals the real trace excess 9^r. It does not hold for arbitrary matrices.

A local quadratic form transforms by Q_v→g_v^−T Q_v g_v^−1, preserving s_v^T Q_v s_v. Connection mismatch Q_w−g_vw^−T Q_v g_vw^−1 is covariant.

On ⊕_v C^9, the connection Hamiltonian has quadratic form JΣ_edges ||ψ_w−U_vw ψ_v||². It is positive and gauge covariant. A consistent unitary representation or explicitly chosen link phases is required. Earlier simulations of a 243-dimensional network and a 36-dimensional square distinguished flat and curved transport; these are model-dependent source-reported results in this synthesis. Local group averaging projects onto invariant states for a genuine group representation. Dynamical links may be represented by ℓ²(G), but the full link space is enormous (648^81).

5. Matter–curvature susceptibility (VIII)

A single plaquette modulo local gauge transformations has 25 conjugacy classes. In normalized class coordinates let u_C=√(|C|/648), L_G=I−uu† and R=diag(R_2(C)). Choose H_λ=J_E L_G+(J_R+γλ)R. The ground-state response for a nondegenerate ground state is

dE_0/dλ=γ⟨R⟩,

d⟨R⟩/dλ=−2γΣ_{n>0}|⟨n|R|0⟩|²/(E_n−E_0).

For several sources, χ_ij=2 Re Σ_{n>0}⟨0|R_i|n⟩⟨n|R_j|0⟩/(E_n−E_0) is PSD. Thus δR=−γχδλ to first order. The sign of γ is a model choice; this is not a universal law of Einstein scalar curvature. The inverse susceptibility motivates a local field equation if its spatial structure can be derived.

6. Scalar interaction and continuum bridge (IX)

Choose weights 4/9,1/9,1/36 for axial, face and body directions respectively, giving the ratio 16:4:1. With spacing h, L_h f=h⁻²Σ_d w_d(f(x)−f(x+hd)). This recovers a scalar Newtonian interaction conditionally; it does not establish equivalence principle or relativistic gravity.

8. Linearized gravitational gauge and constraints (XII–XIII)

Let D_i=(T_i−T_i⁻¹)/(2h). They commute and are skew-adjoint. Kξ_ij=D_iξ_j+D_jξ_i. Define R_ijkl(h)=(D_kD_jh_il+D_lD_ih_jk−D_lD_jh_ik−D_kD_ih_jl)/2. Then RK=0. The linearized spatial Einstein operator G has D_iG_ij=0. For nonzero p its spectrum is 0 (3 gauge), −p²/2 (one transverse scalar), +p²/2 (two TT). Its curvature kernel is exactly the gauge image on nonconstant modes. Constant modes are exceptional.

Spectral reconciliation rescales D_i by √(ℓ_26/Σ p_i²) on nonzero modes to match the weighted Laplacian. This is generally nonlocal. It does not work at extra derivative zeros without modification.

Introduce canonical h,π and C=D_iD_jh_ij−D²tr h, C_i=−2D_jπ_ij. Smearing gives δh_ij=D_iξ_j+D_jξ_i and δπ_ij=−D_iD_jN+δ_ijD²N. All linear constraint brackets vanish. For p≠0 the four independent first-class constraints remove eight of twelve phase dimensions. Thus two canonical pairs survive. In a frame with p along z, constraints and gauge fixing leave h_xx=−h_yy and h_xy, with matching TT momenta.

A quadratic Hamiltonian in suitable units is H=Σ(π:π−(tr π)²/2+h:Gh/2). Its equations preserve the constraints: Cdot=−D_i C_i and C_i dot=0. The TT wave equation is hddot=−p²h. The census is 324−2·104=116=104+12, with twelve homogeneous phase dimensions requiring nonlinear global analysis.

9. Nonlinear relational calculus (XIII–XIV)

On any finite pointwise real function algebra, a derivation satisfying ordinary Leibniz must vanish: for each indicator e, e²=e implies (1−2e)D(e)=0 and the multiplier is invertible. But Δ=(T−I)/h obeys Δ(fg)=(Δf)(Tg)+fΔg. Covariant translation on operator fields T_i X(v)=U_i(v)X(v+e_i)U_i(v)⁻¹ preserves multiplication, giving the same shifted rule.

For vector fields, T_iT_j=K_ij T_jT_i with K_ij=U_i(v)U_j(v+e_i)U_i(v+e_j)⁻¹U_j(v)⁻¹. Thus [∇_i,∇_j]=(K_ij−I)T_jT_i/h². For operator fields replace K by its adjoint action. Six paths across a cube in permutation order 123,213,231,321,312,132 yield relative holonomies P_a P_b⁻¹ whose ordered product telescopes to I. This is the transported multiplicative lattice Bianchi identity.

A candidate continuous Lorentz area-curvature action is κΣ_p ε_abcd Σ_p^ab(log K_p)^cd with simple bivectors Σ=u∧v satisfying Σ∧Σ=0. The finite modular group has no canonical real Lorentz logarithm. A four-dimensional discretization, dual-face pairing and normalization must be specified before this becomes a gravitational action.

Naive vector generators M_f D fail to close: on a three-cycle with f=e_0,g=e_1, their commutator is [[−1,0,1],[0,1,−1],[0,0,0]]/4. Its diagonal is nonzero while every M_bD has zero diagonal.

The remedy is the full crossed product: X_f,r=M_f T_r, with [X_f,r,X_g,s]=M_(f T_r g−g T_s f)T_(r+s). For V=F_3³ this is M_27(R). Elementary operators obey E_ab E_cd=δ_bc E_ad and [E_ab,E_cd]=δ_bc E_ad−δ_da E_cb. Reciprocal edges generate E_aa−E_bb. Hence 729=702+26+1, with traceless closure sl(27,R) and central identity. Complexification admits quantum observables and su(27) as a distinct real form of sl(27,C). A Hermitian relational Hamiltonian generates iħ ρdot=[H,ρ]. Exact matrix closure is not yet nonlinear gravitational closure.

10. Dynamical relational metric (XV)

Let symmetric nonnegative weights define L=Σ_e w_e b_e b_e^T, b_ab=e_a−e_b. For connected graphs, R_ab=b_ab^T L⁺ b_ab is effective resistance. Uniform unit weights give L=27I−11^T and R_ab=2/27 for all distinct pairs. Geometry therefore requires differentiation of weights. The centred Gram matrix is L⁺; spectral coordinates u_α(a)/√λ_α embed the square root of resistance as Euclidean distance. The full embedding has dimension 26, not three.

Heat return P(t)=tr(exp(−tL))/|V| defines d_s(t)=2tΣ λ exp(−tλ)/Σ exp(−tλ). A three-coordinate lattice extension with the weighted symbol above recovers d_s≈3 at intermediate scales; the coordinate dimension was selected, not independently derived.

For a connected updated graph L'=L+δw b_f b_f^T, the exact inverse update on the mean-zero subspace is

L'^+=L⁺−δw L⁺b_f b_f^TL⁺/(1+δw R_f).

Hence R'_e=R_e−δw(b_e^TL⁺b_f)²/(1+δw R_f). In particular R'_f=R_f/(1+δw R_f). The susceptibility χ_ef=(b_e^TL⁺b_f)² is PSD by the Schur product theorem. This is the Generative Metric Response Law. For all 351 edges of the complete graph it is actually positive definite: the b_e b_e^T form a basis of symmetric operators on the 26-dimensional mean-zero subspace, and conjugation by (L⁺)^1/2 is invertible there.

Matter-dependent weights w_ab=w_ab^0+γ⟨n_a+n_b⟩ give a candidate backreaction mechanism. Resistance contraction is not itself gravitational attraction. For a quantum configuration basis |w⟩, define Rhat_ab=Σ_w R_ab(w)|w⟩⟨w|. A superposition has a distance expectation and variance; all these distance operators commute in this representation. A common matter-geometry Hamiltonian is needed for genuine backreaction.

11. Unified architecture and research targets

The programme is a chain of typed bridges: Fibonacci generation → arithmetic area → Weyl phase and Lorentz frame → independent connection → curvature and quantum transport → response → tensor reconstruction → first-class reduction → nonlinear relational operators → dynamical metric. No single bridge establishes the entire physical theory. The central target is a field-dependent constraint representation with a controlled continuum limit, derived from a common microscopic dynamics. The 24-cycle is a candidate internal clock; relational time, causal cones, Lorentzian metric reconstruction and finite propagation remain future work (Investigation XVI). Neither a physical value of ħ nor G nor c has been derived.
