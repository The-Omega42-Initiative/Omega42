## Native mathematical audit and new mechanism
A=[[0,1],[1,1]], F(a,b)=(b,a+b) mod9, A^12=-I, A^24=I mod9. Partition 24+24+24+8+1. F is a permutation, so no inter-orbit attraction, no native nine-step descent, no nonzero-to-zero reset. A^6 commutes with F and cannot switch its orbits. Any invertible linear map fixes zero. A genuine folding adapter needs a separate constrained transition operator, not just F.
Define potential state space X, admissible directed edges E_s depending on sequence s, height H_s, functional subset N_s. A descent operator D_s may choose an admissible neighbor with strictly lower H_s, otherwise stay. On finite X it reaches a local minimum in at most |X|-1 strict moves. This is a candidate formal model, not a solution of Levinthal's paradox.
Define reversible reset with memory R(x,m)=(m,x) on X×X. Starting (x,0) gives (0,x), preserving information in the auxiliary register. R²=I. This is a precise toy model of reset without erasure; no claim chaperones implement it.
Orbit transition via affine translation T_v(x)=x+v: for any x,y choose v=y-x. Invertible, can move between F-orbits, unlike powers of F. Candidate representation of external intervention; chemistry must specify admissibility.
