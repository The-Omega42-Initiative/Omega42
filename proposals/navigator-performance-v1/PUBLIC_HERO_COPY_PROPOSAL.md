# Every question has a place. What will you discover from yours?

Ω42 is an open mathematical and computational framework for navigating questions through a shared structure. It turns the exact wording of a question into a reproducible coordinate, opens four structured directions for exploration, and gives an AI Navigator a common frame for building a reading—while leaving the final judgment with the human Striker.

**One structure. Any question. Your judgment.**

Explore the mathematics. Try the instruments. Challenge the protocol. Build what comes next.

## Status statement

Ω42 is an actively developed open research and computational project. Its finite mathematical results, software computations, interpretations and speculative research directions are identified separately. The protocol is open for independent review and implementation.

## Public invitation

- Explore: ask a question and inspect its coordinate and four directions.
- Challenge: test the arithmetic, challenge an interpretation or find a counterexample.
- Build: implement the protocol independently and test interoperability.
- Contribute: submit a proof audit, reproducible bug report, new instrument or protocol proposal.
