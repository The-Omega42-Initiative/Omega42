Four kinds of statement

Know what kind of thing you are reading

Mathematical fact (CDT-A): established by a valid derivation within stated assumptions, recomputed in the repository. Constructed interpretation (CDT-B): a meaning assigned to a structure inside Omega42. Speculative proposition (CDT-C, exploratory): a possibility explored without proof. External correspondence: a proposed relation to something outside Omega42, which needs its own evidence. Example: the units modulo 9 form a group of order 6 (fact); reading that group as ongoing existence (interpretation); that it describes an aspect of consciousness (speculation); that a physical system instantiates it (correspondence). Part 234 sets this out, with the boundary of the thought experiment and the method for comparing unconventional sources.
