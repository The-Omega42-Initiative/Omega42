Speculative Foundations Program

Explore without boundaries. Investigate with every method.

Ω42 is a thought experiment, and speculation is one of its engines. An idea does not need to be established before it can be explored, and a possibility does not need to be conventional before it can be investigated. Never confuse what cannot currently be demonstrated with what cannot legitimately be explored.

Foundational programs

Where existence meets structure

O42-MOR-01Mathematical Origins of Reality. The idea that reality is mathematical has a long history: Tegmark's Mathematical Universe Hypothesis, ontic structural realism, Wheeler's “it from bit”. Omega42 offers a program rather than a conclusion: one exact finite structure, a repeatable procedure, four layers (structure, dynamics, history, transformation), and one test above the others: can the construct derive something that was not built into it? (Companion 234.15)
O42-PD-01The primordial distinction. Modulo 9, the balance point 4.5 becomes a pair: 4 is minus a half and 5 is plus a half, and their difference is the unit, 5 − 4 = 1. Zero alone stays still forever; zero with one distinction generates a whole orbit. The record holds a balance and a propagation; what they mean for existence is the speculation (Companion 234.17).
O42-ANT-01Four operations on the Antipodes. Added, an Antipodal pair gives 9; subtracted, the separations 7, 5, 3, 1; divided, −1 modulo 9 for every unit digit; multiplied, a product that rises as the separation falls, exactly. And the digit rule is the half-turn of the loop: every state meets its Antipode twelve steps on (DF-004, Companion 234.18).

Every relationship between the construct and another system is entered in one of seven kinds: analogy, structural correspondence, mathematical equivalence, derivation, physical realization, empirical correlation, causal relationship. They are not interchangeable (records/relationships/register.json).

Featured speculation

Why does nature pick Fibonacci?

Of the 54 invertible two-term laws on the numbers modulo nine, sixteen reach the full 24-step loop, and Fibonacci, with coefficients (1, 1), is the smallest of them. Could an economy of coefficients explain which patterns we meet: among laws that give the same longest cycle, the world shows the cheapest? Develop it, challenge it, or propose a rival.
