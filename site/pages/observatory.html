Instrument II · collective observability · computed

Several partial observers, one complete picture.

Instrument IV · reversible memory · proved

A memory that rides the world and can always be undone.

The register Tg(x, m) = (Nx, m + g(x)) is reversible on all 729 configurations. After one revolution of an orbit the memory moves by the orbit sum sO, so every additive memory is classified by five numbers in ℤ/9: 9⁵ = 59,049 classes. CRM-01 (g = a) has every orbit sum 0 and never winds. Set an orbit sum, step forward or back, and watch the period stretch.

Instrument V · depth and renormalization · proved

The same law at every resolution.

The world of 81 states is one level of a tower, (ℤ/3k)², the finite quotients of ℤ₃[φ]. Each nonzero orbit lifts to three orbits of three times the length; the states that look like zero at one depth hold a fixed point and an eight-cycle at the next.
