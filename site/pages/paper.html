The Mathematics of Omega42: one law, an infinite tower of exact worlds.

Part I proves nineteen theorems and two corollaries on orbits, spectra, observability, memory, resonance and geometry. Part II surveys the whole register, from the golden ring at every prime to E₈, the Fibonacci quasicrystal and the quantum cat map. Part III sets out twelve open problems and invites mathematicians to check, extend and join the work. Speculation is set apart and labeled.
