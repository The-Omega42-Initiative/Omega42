01 · Start here

Understanding Omega42

What is Omega42?A thought experiment you can use: one exact mathematical world, a protocol that lets any AI explore a question from a position in that world, and an open record of everything explored.

Omega42 (Ω42) has three layers. The construct is an exact world that begins with 81 states and grows without end, each coordinate three times finer and nine times as many states at every depth of its tower. The protocol, written in the Core Specification, tells an AI Navigator how to place a question in that world and read it. The record is the public repository where navigations, explorations, reviews and research are kept, dated and hashed.

Explore further: The Omega42 paper · Companion, Part 0

What does the coordinate do, and what does it not do?It places your exact wording on the loop. It does not decide the meaning or the truth of your question.

The coordinate is computed from measurable features of the wording: words, letters and syllables. The same words under the same calculation rules always give the same coordinate; different words for the same meaning can give a different one. The Navigator interprets the question and explores the perspectives the coordinate opens. Whether the coordinate adds anything useful to the Navigator's ordinary reasoning is an open research question, to be settled by a registered test with a decoy control.

Explore further: How Omega42 works · Companion 234.14

What is its central idea?That every question can be given a reproducible coordinate in one shared structure, explored from there, and compared with other questions placed in the same structure.

The coordinate is calculated from defined, measurable features of the exact wording, not from an assessment of the question's meaning, truth or importance. That gives different questions a common mathematical reference point; the Navigator then interprets the question and explores what that position may contribute. Changing the wording can change the coordinate, even when the intended meaning stays much the same. Because the structure is fixed and the same for everyone, readings of different questions can be laid side by side, and where questions from unrelated fields land on the same place or on each other's mirror, the congruence register records it. What a reading means is decided by the person who asked.

Explore further: The congruence

Why does it use a mathematical structure?Because arithmetic gives every question a position anyone can recompute, independent of how the AI happens to frame it.

A position computed from the exact wording does not depend on the AI's habits or on anyone's preferences. That makes readings reproducible at their base and comparable across AI systems, and it gives speculation a precise starting point.

Explore further: How Omega42 works

What are the 81 states and the cycles?Pairs of numbers from 0 to 8, moved by the rule (a, b) → (b, a + b) modulo 9. The 81 pairs fall into five loops: three of 24 steps, one of 8, and one fixed point.

This is the Fibonacci rule read modulo 9. Its 24-step period is a classical fact (the Pisano period of 9). The canonical loop starts at (2, 5); each position has a mirror twelve steps away. Construct X runs the same law at every power of three.

Explore further: Construct X · Strike a question

Is it a framework, an AI protocol or a research program?All three, on one foundation.

The mathematics is the framework; the Core Specification is the AI protocol; the library, the Discovery Bulletin, Construct X and the research areas are the program.

The Speculative Foundations Program gives ideas three spaces: exploration (what could be), development (what follows) and examination (what supports it and what counts against it). An idea's freedom to be explored is kept apart from its status.

Explore further: Speculative Foundations

What is a thought experiment?A question pursued by reasoning inside stated assumptions, to see what follows.

Omega42 itself is one: inside the construct the 81-state world is the complete world by definition, and its implications are open to exploration. Omega42 keeps three levels apart: the mathematical construct (proved and computed), the premise that it is a complete world (explored for its consequences), and any correspondence with the physical, biological, psychological or social world (investigated, tested, open to rejection). A thought experiment can stipulate its own world; it does not thereby show that the actual universe is that world.

04 · Mathematics and research

Understanding the foundations

Which parts are mathematically established?The arithmetic of the construct and the tower: periods, orbits, norms and counts, recomputed in the repository's checks.

Each such statement is CDT-A, with a script that recomputes it. Classical results are credited as prior art, and Omega42 asserts priority only for what it adds.

Explore further: Prior art

How do I tell a mathematical fact from an interpretation or a speculation?Every statement carries one of four kinds: mathematical fact, constructed interpretation, speculative proposition or external correspondence.

A fact is established by a valid derivation within stated assumptions; an interpretation is a meaning assigned inside Omega42; a speculation is a possibility explored without proof; an external correspondence is a proposed relation to something outside Omega42, which needs its own evidence.

Explore further: Companion Explorer · Companion 234.10

Has the idea that existence comes from a mathematical structure been researched before?Yes, for centuries. Omega42 adds a specific structure, a repeatable method and a test, not a conclusion.

Tegmark’s Mathematical Universe Hypothesis holds that physical reality is a mathematical structure; ontic structural realism holds that relations come before objects; Wheeler’s “it from bit” puts information first; Platonism and structuralism ask whether structures exist on their own. None is experimentally established. Omega42 keeps four propositions apart: that a structure is well defined, that something exists physically, that reality is the structure, and that the structure explains how existence arises. Its program, O42-MOR-01, asks whether one exact 81-state structure can derive anything that was not built into it (Companion 234.15; Speculative Foundations).

What is exploratory?Interpretations, analogies and speculative connections, marked exploratory or CDT-C.

They are free to be explored and developed. They are not presented as results.

How are mathematical statements checked?By code that runs on every change: the repository's checks recompute them.

A change that breaks a recomputed statement fails the build. Recomputations made while exploring are labeled as such and are not counted as registered tests.

What would count as evidence for a connection?A test whose criterion is fixed before it is run.

The Speculation Test Protocol registers the hypothesis, the criterion and the blinding before the run, and keeps failures as carefully as successes.

How are hypotheses tested?Registered first, then run, with the result kept whatever it is.

See the knowledge register in the repository's records for every registered test and its status.

Can outside researchers reproduce the results?Yes. Every number comes from published scripts, and the release carries its checksums.

The reference script prints its own SHA-256; the repository's checks run publicly on every change.

08 · The mathematics

More about the mathematics

Why digital roots and the number 9?Digital-root arithmetic is arithmetic modulo 9, where the Fibonacci rule closes into a loop of 24.

Read modulo 9, the rule add the last two numbers repeats every 24 steps, and halfway round every state meets its own negative. Modulo 9 is the one power of a single prime with that cycle and that mirror.

Explore further: The paper, Section 2 · Explore with your Navigator →

Why 81 states?A state is a pair of residues modulo 9, and there are 9 × 9 = 81 pairs.

The 81 states fall into exactly five loops: three of 24 steps, one of 8, and the still point (0, 0). Every state lies on exactly one loop.

Explore further: Theorem 2.1 · Explore with your Navigator →

What is special about 2 and 5?They seed the canonical loop, and 2 × 5 = 10 ≡ 1, so each undoes the other modulo 9.

2 and 5 are the only primitive roots modulo 9. The loop from (2, 5) is the canonical cycle 2, 5, 7, 3, 1, 4, 5, 9, 5, 5, 1, 6, 7, 4, 2, 6, 8, 5, 4, 9, 4, 4, 8, 3, selected by frozen criteria over an exhaustive enumeration.

Explore further: The symbols · Explore with your Navigator →

Is the world only 81 states?No. The 81 states are the first floor of an infinite tower.

At depth k there are 9ᵏ states, each coordinate three times finer than the floor below and nine times as many states in all. The tower never ends, and its limit is the plane of 3-adic numbers.

What is the uniqueness proof?A proof that one canonical reading survives a frozen set of criteria applied to every candidate.

Three 24-step loops exist, so uniqueness is not orbital: the criteria K, fixed before testing, leave one survivor among 72 readings. The paper shows exactly where mathematics cannot prefer one candidate and the constitution decides openly.
