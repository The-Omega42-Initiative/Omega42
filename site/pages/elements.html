The elements, read on one loop.

Each element is fixed by one whole number, its atomic number Z. Omega42 reads that number in two exact ways: its digital root (Z modulo 9, with 9 for 0) and its place on the canonical 24-step loop (step Z modulo 24). Colour the table by either reading, then select an element.

Select an element.

A constructed lens. The readings are exact arithmetic on Z and nothing more. Chemistry depends on electron configuration, nuclear structure and energy, which Z modulo 9 does not determine. No stability, reactivity or property is inferred from a residue. The noble gases have atomic numbers 2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86 and 118, with digital roots 2, 1, 9, 9, 9, 5 and 1: a pattern to notice, not a law.
