Nine points. One loop.

The emblem brings the Omega42 loop into dialogue with the traditional nine-point figure known as the Enneagram. The two structures have distinct origins and constructions, while their mathematical relationships open a rich field of comparison: recurrence, symmetry, modular arithmetic, transformation and the geometry of process.

The emblem

The nine-point figure (gold triangle, violet six-point path) with the Omega42 loop drawn through it: the first twelve steps in cyan, the next twelve in magenta. The two halves are mirror images, and that is exact.

Mathematical fact

The triangle joins 3, 6 and 9: the multiples of three, which Omega42 calls the 3-layer (9 is 0 when you count modulo nine). The loop lands on the triangle every fourth step and only then, because a Fibonacci number is divisible by 3 exactly when its index is divisible by 4.

Mathematical fact

The path 1 → 4 → 2 → 8 → 5 → 7 → 1 follows the repeating digits of 1/7 = 0.142857142857… Its six digits are exactly the numbers that have an inverse modulo nine, the units. The triangle and the path divide the nine positions the way Omega42's arithmetic divides them. The order of the path comes from 1/7, not from the Omega42 law.

Mathematical fact

The canonical loop: add the last two numbers and keep the remainder after dividing by nine. 9, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 4, 3, 7, 1, 8, then 9, 8, 8, 7, 6, 4, 1, 5, 6, 2, 8, 1, and round again. Each digit and the digit twelve steps on add to 9 or to 0. Of the loop's 19 distinct chords, 4 are lines of the nine-point figure, all on the six-point path. This alignment opens a combinatorial investigation into how recurrence structures meet the geometry of the nine-point figure.

Mathematical fact

The Antipode of a digit is 9 minus it: 1 and 8, 2 and 7, 3 and 6, 4 and 5. On the figure the Antipode is the left-right mirror, and the whole nine-point figure is symmetric under it. So is the loop: its second half is the mirror of its first, because twelve steps of the law turn every state into its negative (T¹² = −I modulo 9).

Mathematical fact

Doubling from 1, modulo nine, gives 1, 2, 4, 8, 7, 5 and back to 1: the same six digits as the path of 1/7, visited in another order. In Omega42, doubling is the move that turns one voice of the law into the next (Companion 52.2).

The Enneagram

A diagram of process

G. I. Gurdjieff taught the nine-pointed figure to students in Russia from around 1916, as a figure of process and transformation: the triangle and the six-point path drew his laws of three and of seven. P. D. Ouspensky recorded the teaching in In Search of the Miraculous, published in 1949. The nine personality types associated with the figure today are a later, separate development, begun by Oscar Ichazo in the 1960s and elaborated by Claudio Naranjo from the 1970s. Omega42 uses the geometry of the figure as a point of comparison. It is not based on Gurdjieff's teaching and does not endorse the personality system.

The Omega42 structure

Eighty-one states, one law

Every pair of digits (a, b) moves to (b, a + b), keeping the remainder after dividing by nine. The 81 pairs fall into five loops: one still point, one loop of 8, and three of 24. The emblem draws the first digit of the canonical 24-step loop; the full world is the 81 states and what a question opens in it.

The open question

What deeper principles connect different mathematics of recurrence?

The nine-point figure and the Omega42 loop divide the nine positions in the same way and share the same mirror, although one is built from 1/7 and the other from the Fibonacci law. Is that because any figure on nine positions that respects multiplication modulo nine must do so, or does it point to something more particular?
