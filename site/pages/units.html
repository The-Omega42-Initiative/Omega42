How it is kept right

Two derivations that must agree

The unit table stores each factor as an exact decimal where the unit is defined exactly: the inch is 0.0254 m, the pound 0.45359237 kg, the US gallon 231 cubic inches, the electronvolt 1.602176634 × 10⁻¹⁹ J. A separate program derives every factor again from those definitions in exact fractions, without reading the stored value, and checks them against each other with fixtures of known answers, on every change to the repository (python3 site/test/units_check.py).

Sources of the definitions: the BIPM SI Brochure (9th edition) and NIST Special Publication 811. The display uses ordinary floating point; where more than 15 significant figures matter, use the exact factor shown. The calorie is the thermochemical calorie and the BTU the International Table BTU; other variants differ slightly.
