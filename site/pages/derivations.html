1. Orbit architecture: 81 = 24 + 24 + 24 + 8 + 1

Result. N is a bijection of S with five orbits: three of length 24, one of length 8 and the fixed point (0, 0).

Proof. det A = −1 is a unit mod 9, so N is invertible. Modulo 3 the nonzero vectors form one orbit of length 8 (the Pisano period π(3) = 8). Over a state x ≢ 0 mod 3 the period is a multiple of 8 dividing π(9) = 24; since A⁸ ≡ I + 3B (mod 9) with B = (1 1; 1 2) invertible mod 3, A⁸ fixes no such x, so the period is 24, and the 72 such states form three orbits. The 9 states with both coordinates divisible by 3 are 3·(ℤ/3)², a copy of the mod-3 world: one fixed point and one 8-cycle. Checks R0, R5.

2. The antipode: A¹² = −I

Proof. Fₙ₊₁Fₙ₋₁ − Fₙ² = (−1)ⁿ and Aⁿ = (Fₙ₋₁ Fₙ; Fₙ Fₙ₊₁). F₁₂ = 144 ≡ 0, F₁₁ = 89 ≡ 8 ≡ −1, F₁₃ = 233 ≡ 8 ≡ −1 (mod 9), so A¹² ≡ −I and A²⁴ ≡ I. Every state meets its negative twelve steps along its own orbit. Check R5.

3. The golden-ratio ring and the norm

Result. Identify (a, b) with a + bφ where φ² = φ + 1. Then φ(a + bφ) = b + (a + b)φ: the law is multiplication by φ. The quadratic form Q(a, b) = a² + ab − b² is the norm N(a + bφ), and Q(Nx) = −Q(x) because N(φ) = −1.

Consequence. Q² is invariant; it takes the values 1, 4 and 7 on the three 24-orbits and 0 on the 8-cycle and the fixed point, so (period, Q²) separates all five orbits. Checks R5, R7.

4. The tower, depth and renormalization

Result. 3 is inert in ℚ(√5), so Rₖ = ℤ₃[φ]/3ᵏ = (ℤ/3ᵏ)[φ] and the levels of the tower are its finite quotients. At depth k there are (3ᵏ + 1)/2 orbits. Reduction π: Rₖ₊₁ → Rₖ commutes with N; each nonzero orbit of length L lifts to exactly three orbits of length 3L, and the fiber over 0 is the fixed point plus an 8-cycle.

Proof sketch. φ⁸ = 13 + 21φ = 1 + 3(4 + 7φ) with 4 + 7φ a unit, so cubing raises the 3-adic valuation of φ^(8·3ʲ) − 1 by exactly one; orders triple at each level. Counting states gives the three lifts. The fiber over 0 is 3·Rₖ ≅ Rₖ₋₁. Check R7 (3 → 9 → 27 → 81).

Spectral inheritance. The harmonic multiplicities at depth k are m_l = [l = 0] + Σⱼ 3ʲ⁻¹[3ᵏ⁻ʲ ∣ l]. From 81 to 729 states, 48 new harmonics appear, each with multiplicity 9 (432 dimensions), and the 24 inherited harmonics gain 216 dimensions. Fiber averaging J*J splits every field into an inherited part and a detail part, orthogonally. Check R7.

Domain Zero, differentiated. No state returns to 0 under the invertible law. Yet one level deeper, what reads as 0 holds a fixed point and an eight-cycle: apparent nothingness contains unresolved structure.

6. Collective observability

Result. Three observers of a mod 7 at phases 0, 2 and 5 together determine every state from a single round. Of the 253 anchored triples (0, d, e), exactly 24 do. Two observers at phases 0 and d need 5, 4, 3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5 rounds for d = 1, …, 23. Checks R1c, R8.

Depth bound. At depth k, n = 9ᵏ states need at least ⌈log₇ n⌉ = 2, 3, 4, 5 seven-symbol readings for k = 1, …, 4; independent observer maps attain it by base-7 digits. Whether shared phase observers attain it beyond depth 2 is open (investigation X-OBS-02).

7. Spectral architecture and the 281-dimensional algebra

Result. U has eigenvalues e^(2πij/24) with multiplicity m_j = 3 + [3 ∣ j] + [j = 0]: each 24-orbit contributes every 24th root once, the 8-cycle the roots with 3 ∣ j, the fixed point j = 0. The operators commuting with U form M₅ ⊕ 7·M₄ ⊕ 16·M₃, of complex dimension 25 + 112 + 144 = 281. U¹² has eigenspaces of dimensions 41 (+1) and 40 (−1). Checks R2, R7.

8. The coherence operator

Definition. L = 2I − U − U*, Cα = (I + αL/4)⁻¹, α ≥ 0.

Proof. U is unitary and normal, so L and Cα are functions of U. On a harmonic with eigenvalue λ = e^(2πij/24), L acts by 2 − λ − λ̄ = 4 sin²(πj/24), so Cα acts by c_j = 1/(1 + α sin²(πj/24)) ∈ (0, 1]. Hence Cα commutes with U, is self-adjoint, positive and a contraction, fixes every invariant field (c₀ = 1), and lowers the Dirichlet energy ⟨f, Lf⟩. At α = 4 the fastest harmonic keeps 1/5. Check R2.

9. Multiplicity mixing

Result. Any unitary V inside the commutant, block-diagonal on the eigenspaces, commutes with U and preserves the energy of a field in every harmonic. Example: swap the normalized constant field on the fixed point with that on a 24-orbit (both are j = 0 vectors). It changes which orbit carries the energy and keeps every harmonic profile: law-preserving generation of alternatives. For a generic field the stabilizer in the product of unitary groups has dimension Σ(m_j − 1)² = 143, its orbit 281 − 143 = 138. Check R9.

10. Reversible memory and its 59,049 classes

Result. T_g(x, m) = (Nx, m + g(x)) is a bijection of S × ℤ/9 for every g. Running once round an orbit O adds the orbit sum s_O = Σ_{x∈O} g(x). Changing g by a coboundary f∘N − f leaves every orbit sum unchanged, and a g with all orbit sums 0 is a coboundary (solve along the orbit), so the classes are exactly (ℤ/9)⁵ = 59,049.

CRM-01. g = a gives m − b invariant: g = b∘N − b is a coboundary, all orbit sums are 0, and the register records nothing the world does not hold. A unit sum on one 24-orbit makes one cycle of length 24 · 9 = 216. Under deepening, the pullback g∘π multiplies nonzero orbit sums by 3: from depth 2 to 3 it has image 729 and kernel 81, so winding information can be lost by refinement. Checks R3, R7.

11. Why 42

Result. A hyperbolic triangle with angles π/p, π/q, π/r has area π(1 − 1/p − 1/q − 1/r). Among all integer triples with 1/p + 1/q + 1/r < 1 the largest sum is 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/7 = 41/42, so the (2, 3, 7) triangle has the smallest area of any hyperbolic triangle group, π/42 (curvature −1). Check R5.

12. The other primes

Result. Modulo 7 the same matrix has order 16 (x² = x + 1 has no root mod 7, so ℤ/7[φ] is the field of 49 elements and its 48 nonzero elements form three orbits of 16). Modulo 11, where 5 is a square, x² = x + 1 has roots 8 and 4 of orders 10 and 5, and 121 = 1 + 2·5 + 11·10. Checks R5, R7.

13. The Aperture arithmetic

1/7 ≡ 4 (mod 9) since 7 · 4 = 28 ≡ 1; 7 · 7 = 49 ≡ 1 (mod 24); 2 · 5 ≡ 1 (mod 9), so seed 5 runs seed 2's cycle 1 → 2 → 4 → 8 → 7 → 5 backwards; 5 + 4 ≡ 0 (mod 9); 5 + 7 = 12, the antipode of the 24-loop. The digit sequence from the seeds (2, 5) has period 24 and sum 117, and its terms twelve apart sum to 9, or to 18 where both are 9. Check R5.
