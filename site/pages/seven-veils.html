The Seven Veils find the hidden state

One of the 81 states is hidden. You can look at it only through a veil: at any phase t you choose, you see one of seven symbols, the first coordinate of At(x) read mod 7. Each look costs a move. Narrow the candidates, then pick the hidden state on the grid.

The rules are exact Omega42 mathematics: A(a, b) = (b, a + b mod 9) and h(a, b) = a mod 7. The story is a creative frame. Three well-chosen looks are always enough (CB-035, checked on all 81 states).

The 81 states (a, b)

Every state lies on one of five orbits of A (lengths 24, 24, 24, 8, 1), so phases repeat after 24 steps. A look at phase t sees the first coordinate of At(a, b), which is the Fibonacci-type combination F(t−1)·a + F(t)·b mod 9, then read mod 7. Symbols 0 and 1 each cover 18 states (values 0, 7 and 1, 8 mod 9), symbols 2 to 6 cover 9. Looks at phases 0, 2 and 5 separate all 81 states; of all 253 three-phase choices that start at 0, exactly 24 do.
