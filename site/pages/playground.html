The 81-state explorer

Every state, every loop

Show the mathematics
  • The law: T(a, b) = (b, a + b), keeping the remainder after dividing by 9. Its inverse: T⁻¹(a, b) = (b − a, a).
  • The 81 states fall into five loops: one still point (0, 0), one loop of 8 inside the 3-layer, and three loops of 24.
  • Twelve steps of the law turn every state into its negative (T¹² = −I modulo 9), so each state meets its Antipode half a period on.
  • Numbers on the grid count steps from the selected state; the outlined cell is its Antipode.
