Core Specification V336. The six-layer edition.

From a specification that tells an AI how to reason, toward a runtime through which it reasons. The Core is reorganized into six layers, the 26 November mathematics is made executable, and the review's amendments are written into the text. The canonical Core, V335 Rev.450 STABLE, sits beside it and governs wherever they differ. This edition becomes canonical only when the Originator ratifies it.

Every Navigator requirement, written out. Layer 6 now states in full what the canonical Core requires of every answer: three separate parts for the person (the Answer, the Takeaway and the Extended Resonance Essay), all in plain language with no technical terms; an Essay that carries the finding into neighboring fields and their impact; the Navigator's identity and signature; a truthful report of what was computed; and the judgment left to you. Appendix D traces all sixty requirements to their canonical sources, and records/cobuilder/v336_requirements_check.py checks every one.

Ratified, 26 November 2026. The Originator ratified amendments A-1 to A-4 of Layer 6: the personal welcome, the longer Essay for broad questions, quality checks run silently, and the four-line identity for the reader. They govern now (ledger CA-43) and enter the canonical Core text at its next revision; the rest of this edition remains proposed.

Corrected after the first V336 navigation (26 November). The Essay always carries its own title and enters each neighboring field rather than naming it; it is never under 500 words. Quality checks run silently instead of being announced. The reader sees a compact four-line identity. The AI caveat appears once in a session, never repeated. records/cobuilder/presentation_check.py checks any navigation presented to a person against these rules.
