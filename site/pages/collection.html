Where Omega42 came from.

Omega42 grew through thousands of drafts, books and papers kept in the Originator's drives. Selected documents now enter the Library as historical editions. Each one is checked for private material, its mathematics is recomputed by a script anyone can run, and an apparatus says what carries forward into the current release, what was corrected and what was retired.

LIB-01 · Philosophical foundations and the first formalisms

Six documents, each statement placed

Four philosophical papers from September 2026 (Google Drive) and two formal pieces from the 2025 book material (OneDrive). Most of their arithmetic holds. Where a statement does not hold as written, needed restating, or comes without a derivation, the edition says so, beside the computation.

How an edition is made

Four steps before a document enters

1Selected. One family at a time, by the archive policy, on the Originator's instruction.
2Checked. No personal name, place or address; personal and biographical material is withheld.
3Recomputed. Every arithmetic and geometric statement is computed again and marked: holds, restated, does not hold, or no derivation.
4Placed. A concordance says where each idea now lives in the Core and the Companion.
