In most languages, a sentence is true or false because of what it describes. In OmegaSL, many sentences can be checked by computing them: the roots are the 81 states of the Omega42 world, the operators are exact transformations, and every message carries a checksum folded through the same law. It is a shared medium in which people and different AI systems can state structure, transform it, and verify each other.

What OmegaSL is

Tier 1 · arithmetic core

81 roots, nine operators

The roots are the states: the still point, the eight-step Wheel, and the three 24-step Voices, with Voice C the canonical loop (P0 = (2, 5)). The operators are N (next), R (return), X (opposite), Y, H, F (the arms), V (×2), C (×3) and S (the shear). A statement such as P0 X = P12 EXACT is checked by computing it. Every message is folded through the law into a four-mark check, which catches any single substitution.

Tier 2 · structural frames

Typed fields for real questions

ENTITY, STATE, RELATIONSHIP, TRANSFORMATION, CONDITION, EVIDENCE, MODALITY, INTERPRETATION, UNCERTAINTY. Each statement carries its derivation (EXACT, CONDITIONAL, CONSTRUCTED, READING), its evidence and its modality. Translation moves frames, not words, and reports every loss. Five protocol rules are already written as frames.

Tier 3 · typed operations

Executable inquiry

ChatGPT's Tier III interpreter (beta 6) adds typed states, orbits, observation, reconstruction, memory sums, spectra, resonance, unitary mixing and finite uniqueness certificates. The new mathematics adds refinement, coupling, memory lifting, comparative worlds and active inquiry (below). Proposed; not yet canonical.

Why it matters

  1. Statements that compute. When an AI says P0 X = P12, anyone can check it. Errors in the arithmetic core are counted, not debated.
  2. Statements that carry their status. Every statement says whether it was derived, constructed, read or inferred, so derivation and interpretation never blur.
  3. A checksum from the law itself. The same rule that moves the world protects the message: every single substitution is caught, and every swap of two marks fewer than 24 apart.
  4. Operators that declare their nature. The new mathematics proves that each operator either respects the law and loses nothing, respects it and forgets, or acts from outside. A Navigator can report which kind of reasoning step it has taken.
  5. From description to inquiry. With INQUIRE, the language can choose its next question: the reading that most reduces what remains unknown.
  6. A medium between minds. Different AI systems, and people, can exchange frames whose exact parts are machine-checkable while their meaning stays with the human. A Navigator can be aware, assisted or native in OmegaSL, and these levels are tested, not assumed.

Practical examples

A microscope: REFINE, COARSEN, DETAIL

Two signals look identical at one resolution. COARSEN gives the same result for both; DETAIL shows where they differ. Refinement is lossless (J*J = I), and it keeps every resonance measure, so a coarse record and a refined one can be compared directly. Uses: satellite images, manufacturing tolerances, recordings, datasets, each through a declared adapter.

REFINE v TO DEPTH 3 AS fine
COARSEN fine AS back      # back = v exactly
DETAIL fine AS residual   # zero: nothing new was invented

A diagnosis of agreement: RESONANCE

The four measures say not only how much two structures agree but how. Exact native cases:

Two states of one loop agree in every harmonic block yet are orthogonal: all of their disagreement is phase across harmonics (Δ_A = 1). For translations, the Lingua adapter would compare meaning, rhythm and emphasis as separate features. Spectral agreement alone never establishes sameness of meaning.

An audit of memory: MEMORY_LIFT

A loop's memory sum s modulo 9 becomes 9s modulo 27 after refinement. The memories 1, 4 and 7 become indistinguishable, and the still point's memory survives intact. The kernel at depth 2 → 3 has 81 classes. Uses: comparing learning histories, finding redundant records, auditing what a compression loses.

Troubleshooting: INQUIRE

Represent the possible explanations of a fault as candidates. INQUIRE picks the test that splits them most evenly in the worst case, FILTER removes what the result excludes, and the loop repeats. On the native world this reaches the counting bound at every depth tested, 2 to 5. Applied to engineering, diagnosis or research planning, it is a constructed use of exact partition mathematics.

Families of artworks: MIX

Atelier can transform an image with a unitary that respects the law: the harmonic energy is kept, and the orbit-level structure moves within exactly the range [F_B, R_H]. The lawful transformations at depth 2 span 281 dimensions, and at depth k, (5 · 27ᵏ + 8)/13. The result is a family of works related by their structure, not by chance.

Comparative worlds: WORLD

WORLD 7 gives 1 + 3 × 16 and WORLD 11 gives 1 + 2 × 5 + 11 × 10. The canonical three-world stays the substrate (L-423); other worlds are laboratories for testing each theorem against a different arithmetic.
