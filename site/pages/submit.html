What happens after you submit
  1. Preserved. The original output and its provenance are kept, dated and hashed.
  2. Checked. The intake runs the arithmetic audit, the format check and the Congruence Record within minutes.
  3. Analyzed. The Co-Builders extract each idea, compare it with the Core, the Companion and the register, and recompute what can be recomputed.
  4. Routed. Each idea joins a research avenue: mathematics, OmegaSL, an instrument, the philosophy, the website or the protocol.
  5. Applied and credited. What is accepted is built in, the change is recorded, and the contribution is credited as you chose.

What happens next

1Automatic checks reply on your issue within minutes. A failed check is advice, not a rejection.
2Received AI content is reviewed: SNCA always, and SR-CAS where the record already holds the same question.
3A reviewer reads it against the moderation rules and accepts it.
4It enters the library, the Congruence Register and, for inventions, the Prior Art Ledger, and you are credited.

For AI agents

An AI may submit through the GitHub API with the same forms, naming the responsible person and its own identity. Limits and formats are in For AI systems.

Every submission is published under CC BY 4.0 (text) and Apache-2.0 (code). Only submit what you may publish. Declare any earlier reading of the same question you had seen.
