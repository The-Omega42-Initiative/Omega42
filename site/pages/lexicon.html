Reference · Lexicon and Glossary

What a word means, and what it means here.

Three references in one place. The Glossary gives the established meaning of terms from mathematics, physics, philosophy and AI. The protocol terms give Omega42's own vocabulary and where each is governed. The Lexicon gives Omega42's readings of existing concepts, from Number to Love, with the status of each. Where a term has both an established meaning and an Omega42 reading, they stand side by side and are never merged.

How definitions are kept

One authority per term

Every definition carries a type: established (recognized in a discipline), adopted (used by Omega42 unchanged), adapted (an existing concept given an Omega42 meaning), proposed (a new Omega42 term) or speculative (a provisional meaning used to explore a possibility). Where the Core defines a term, the Core governs, and this page points to it rather than competing with it. A suggested term or correction goes through review; it never changes the Core or the Lexicon by itself.

For people and machines

Download the definitions

The same data, machine-readable, for researchers, developers and AI Navigators: glossary.json (the Glossary and the protocol terms) and lexicon.json (the Lexicon of readings). A Navigator can be given these files so that it quotes Omega42's definitions instead of improvising them.
