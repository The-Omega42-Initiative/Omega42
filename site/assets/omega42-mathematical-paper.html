Central construction

Let Sₖ = (ℤ/3ᵏ)² and A(a,b) = (b,a+b). The determinant is −1, so the map is invertible. Multiplication by φ in the ring φ²=φ+1 gives this action, and the norm Q=a²+ab−b² changes sign. At modulus 9 the orbit census is 1+8+3·24=81. The order at modulus 3ᵏ is 8·3ᵏ⁻¹; the finite levels form a compatible inverse system. A primitive state has that full period; the orbit census follows by counting valuation strata. This is the native substrate from which the extensions below depart.

Significance and research directions

The common action supplies a precise laboratory for reversible information, invariant classification, multiresolution analysis and structural correspondence. A commuting diagram is not by itself a strong equivalence: the map may discard information. Observability asks what can be recovered; spectral analysis asks what persists; memory extensions ask what accumulates; geometry asks which representations preserve the law. These are complementary questions, not interchangeable measures.

Prospective applications include sensor placement, reversible computing, coding, constrained generation, signal processing, geometric reconstruction and cross-domain model comparison. Research directions include optimal shared observers at greater depths, complete trace-map period theorems, exact spectral scaling and typed geometric adapters. These are research proposals, not established application performance.
