4. Builder and Navigator controls

  • Instrument selection is deterministic and inspectable.
  • Exact integer orbit calculations use the existing BigInt engine.
  • Temporal calculations inherit the K2 floating-point limitation.
  • Graph validation checks structure and dependencies, not source authenticity or proof validity.
  • Computed records cannot be silently promoted to proved.
  • All user-provided text is rendered with textContent, not HTML injection.
