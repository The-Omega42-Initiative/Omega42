## Proof

Because d²≡0 mod N, the operators W_(da,db) equal Z^(da)X^(db) and multiply without a Weyl phase. The finite group average P_N is therefore a self-adjoint idempotent (the orthogonal projector onto the common fixed subspace of X^d and Z^d). Its trace is N/9, since only W_(0,0)=I has nonzero trace among the nine distinct Weyl operators.

The projector factorizes as

\[
P_N=\left(\frac{I+Z^d+Z^{2d}}3\right)
\left(\frac{I+X^d+X^{2d}}3\right).
\]

The first factor restricts to basis indices j divisible by 3; the second averages each three-cycle j,j+d,j+2d. Since d divisible by 3 for k>=2, the two factors commute. The joint fixed space has the stated basis v_t, with disjoint supports and dimension N/9.

\[

This proves invariance and commutation. QED.
