# Exact all-depth obstruction to the identity-augmented primitive Weyl sector

**Proposal-only theorem, 23 November 2026.** This closes the question explicitly left open in WEYL_COMMUTANT_FOURIER_PROJECTIONS_2026-11-23.md.

Let N=3^k, k>=2, and R=Z/N. Let W_x (x∈R²) be the odd-N Weyl basis with

W_x W_y = exp(2πi inv2·ω(x,y)/N) W_(x+y),

where inv2 is the inverse of 2 modulo N and ω((a,b),(c,d))=ad−bc. Let Q(a,b)=a²+ab−b². Write

S={x∈R²: Q(x)²=1 mod N},
E=Σ_(x∈S)W_x.

By the pure Fibonacci orbit classification (MF-184/MF-55), S is the union of the two primitive A²-orbits with norms ±1, so E belongs to the parity-even primitive Weyl sector V_even.

## Theorem

For z=(3^(k−1),0)∈R², the coefficient of W_z in E² is **exactly −3^(k−1)**. In particular, the linear space span{I}⊕V_even is not closed under operator multiplication for every k>=2.

## Proof

Set t=3^(k−1), so t²≡0 mod N and t≠0. The coefficient in question is

c_z=Σ_(x∈S, z−x∈S) exp(2πi inv2·ω(x,z−x)/N).

Write x=(a,b), y=z−x=(t−a,−b). Then ω(x,y)=−bt, and

Q(y)=Q(x)−t(2a+b) (mod N).

Because Q(x) is a unit, squaring gives

Q(y)²−Q(x)² ≡ −2t Q(x)(2a+b) (mod N).

Thus, for x∈S, the condition y∈S is equivalent to 2a+b≡0 mod3.

For each nonzero residue pair (a0,b0) mod3, Q(a0,b0)≠0 mod3, and the congruence Q(a,b)²=1 mod3^k has **exactly 3^(k−1)** lifts x reducing to (a0,b0). To see this, note Q(a,b)²≡1 mod3 automatically for every primitive pair, and its gradient 2Q(a,b)(2a+b,a−2b) is nonzero modulo3: the two linear forms cannot vanish simultaneously on a primitive pair because the matrix [[2,1],[1,−2]] has determinant −5≡1 mod3. At each lift step from modulus 3^s to 3^(s+1), exactly three of the nine coordinate-digit choices preserve the congruence.

The condition 2a+b=0 mod3 selects exactly the two primitive residues (a,b)=(1,1) and (2,2) mod3. Each contributes 3^(k−1) solutions. Their Weyl phases are exp(−2πi inv2·b/3), which are the two nontrivial cube roots of unity (since inv2≡2 mod3). Their sum is −1. Therefore

\[
\boxed{c_z=3^{k−1}(\exp(2πi/3)+\exp(4πi/3))=−3^{k−1}.}
\]

The vector z is nonzero but nonprimitive for k>=2. Every element of span{I}⊕V_even has Weyl support only at 0 and primitive vectors. Since the Weyl operators are linearly independent, E² lies outside that span. QED.

## Exceptional level k=1

At N=3, the parity-even primitive sector is one-dimensional, generated by E=Σ_(x≠0)W_x. In the standard Weyl convention, direct Weyl multiplication gives E²=8I−2E, so span{I,E} is a two-dimensional unital *-subalgebra. This exceptional closure does **not** extend to any k>=2.

## Consequences

1. The exact shear Fourier projections into V_even are Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal, but adjoining I does not make their top-level range an operator subalgebra for k>=2.
2. Hence the trace-preserving Hilbert–Schmidt projection onto span{I}⊕V_even cannot be a quantum conditional expectation onto a C*-subalgebra at k>=2.
3. This does not rule out a larger, nontrivial algebra generated by V_even or a different module/intertwining construction.

## Verification

The companion test enumerates S and computes the coefficient c_z by exact cyclotomic exponent multiplicities for k=2,3,4. Its exact result is the integer −3^(k−1), obtained from the two nontrivial cube-root multiplicities. The all-depth theorem is the argument above, not the finite tests.
