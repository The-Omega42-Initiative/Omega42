# Independent orbit-completeness proof for depth-r shear (MF-43)

**Proposal-only, 23 November 2026.** Companion to MF43_LAYER_LIFT_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_2026-11-23.md. Establishes the missing orbit-transitivity step by an explicit congruence-kernel argument. This is an independent reproof of an existing canonical theorem, not a claim of a new foundational discovery.

Write R_k=Z/3^k, A(a,b)=(b,a+b), B_r(a,b)=(a,b+3^r a), 1<=r<=k. Let H_(k,r) be the full determinant-one principal congruence subgroup, proved to be generated by A-conjugates of B_r in the preceding audit.

## Lemma — congruence-kernel transitivity on primitive lifts

For any primitive vectors x,y in R_k² with x≡y (mod 3^r), there is M in H_(k,r) such that Mx=y.

**Proof (constructive).** Since x is primitive, one coordinate is a unit. Complete x to a basis (x,z) of R_k² with determinant 1: if x=(a,b) and a is a unit, choose z=(0,a^(-1)); if b is a unit, choose z=(-b^(-1),0). Write y=αx+βz. The congruence y≡x mod 3^r means α≡1 and β≡0 mod 3^r; in particular α is a unit. Define a matrix in the basis (x,z) by

\[
T=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&0\\\beta&\alpha^{-1}\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Then T has determinant 1, sends the first basis vector to (α,β), and T≡I mod 3^r. Conjugating by the basis-change matrix gives M∈H_(k,r) with Mx=y. QED.

## Theorem — full depth-r orbit classification

For a nonzero x=3^j u modulo 3^k, let n=k-j and h=min(r,n). Two nonzero states x,y are in the same orbit under G_(k,r)=<A,B_r> if and only if:

1. Their coordinate valuations j coincide; and
2. Their primitive parts u,v satisfy Q(u)²≡Q(v)² mod 3^h.

**Proof.** Both A and B_r preserve coordinate valuation. Reduction of G_(k,r) modulo 3^h is generated by A, since B_r≡I mod 3^h. Hence the stated invariant is necessary, by Q(Au)=-Q(u).

For sufficiency, identify valuation-j states with primitive vectors modulo 3^n. By the established MF-55 finite Fibonacci orbit classification at modulus 3^h (also following from MF-39), equality of norm squares implies the reductions of u and v modulo 3^h are related by A^t for some integer t. Replace u by A^t u. We now have u≡v mod 3^h. If n<=r, then h=n and equality already holds. If n>r, h=r and the preceding transitivity lemma, applied at level n, supplies an element of H_(n,r) taking u to v. By the independent group-generation audit, H_(n,r) is contained in G_(n,r), so the required transformation exists. Finally the same word in A,B_r acts on x=3^j u modulo 3^k and produces y=3^j v. QED.
