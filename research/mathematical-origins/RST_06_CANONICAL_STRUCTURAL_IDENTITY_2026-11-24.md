# RST-06 canonical orbit identity
24 November 2026. Research only.
For nonzero x let G(x)=conj(x_p)x/|x_p|, p first nonzero coordinate. G removes global phase. For unit x define C(x)=lexicographic minimum of G(V^m x), m=0,...,L-1, using interleaved real and imaginary coordinates. Finite orbit sets coincide under phase and evolution; equal minima imply intersecting orbits. Thus C(x)=C(y) iff quotient distance is zero. Canonicalization is not necessarily continuous; exact equality must not be confused with rounded numerical keys.
Prototype in spectral coordinates uses largest magnitude pivot (first index in ties), phase normalization, quantized lexicographic minimum at nine decimal places. Local execution at N=3,9,27,81,243: ten equivalent and ten randomized-phase comparisons per dimension passed (100 comparisons). Uses predicted occupied indices, not independent spectrum verification. Quantization may collide or split equivalent states near boundaries; use RST-05 for candidate verification. O(NL) work after spectral conversion. No CI claim.
