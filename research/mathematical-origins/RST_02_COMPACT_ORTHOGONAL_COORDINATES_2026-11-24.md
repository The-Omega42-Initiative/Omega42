# RST-02 compact orthogonal coordinates (2026-11-24)
For N=3^k, k>=2, d=N/3, apply the real orthogonal Helmert matrix with rows (1,1,1)/sqrt(3), (1,-1,0)/sqrt(2), (1,1,-2)/sqrt(6) to each triple x[t+vd], v=0,1,2. Keep both contrast rows and common-mode entries t not divisible by 3 as detail coordinates (2d+2d/3=8N/9). Recurse on common-mode entries t divisible by 3, which equal J* x for the established stabilizer embedding J. Stop at N=1 or 3. The inverse inserts the recursively synthesized common modes and applies the transpose Helmert matrix. Each stage is a permutation and orthogonal block transform; induction proves a unitary N-to-N transform, with detail spaces precisely the orthogonal stabilizer layers. Work O(N) by geometric summation; output storage N complex coefficients, not compression below N. Individual coordinates within a layer need not be cat-map eigenvectors. Numerical local execution at k=1..10 (N up to 59049) gave maximum reconstruction norm error 2.966e-16 and squared-norm error 4.441e-16. No CI claim. Proposal only.
