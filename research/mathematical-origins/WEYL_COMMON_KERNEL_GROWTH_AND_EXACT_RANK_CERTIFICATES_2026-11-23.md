# All-depth common-kernel growth and exact ranks at N=9,27,81

**23 November 2026.** This strengthens WEYL_ALL_DEPTH_COMMON_ANNIHILATOR_THEOREM_2026-11-23.md. It proves an all-depth lower bound and exact dimensions at three levels, without extrapolating finite calculations to an all-depth equality.

Let N=3^k (k>=2), d=N/3, Q(a,b)=a²+ab−b², and W_(a,b)|j>=ζ^(a(j+b)−2^(-1)ab)|j+b>, ζ=exp(2πi/N). For unit norm q define F_q=Σ_(Q(a,b)=q)W_(a,b); for unit square s define E_s=Σ_(Q(a,b)²=s)W_(a,b). Put

\[
K_N=\bigcap_{s\in(R^\times)^2}\ker E_s,\qquad
v_t=|3t\rangle+|3t+d\rangle+|3t+2d\rangle
\quad(0\le t<N/9).
\]

## Theorem A (all depths)

For every k>=2 and every unit q, F_q v_t=0 for every 0<=t<N/9. Hence E_s v_t=0 for all unit square s, and

\[
\boxed{\dim_\mathbb C K_N\ge N/9=3^{k-2}.}
\]

**Proof.** Fix t, let j_0=3t and output coordinate r. In the coefficient of F_q v_t, write the three inputs as j=j_0+vd (v∈F_3), put b_0=r−j_0, b=b_0−vd, and write a=a_0+ud (u∈F_3, a_0 modulo d). Since d²≡0 mod N,

\[
Q(a,b)\equiv Q_0+d\{(2a_0+b_0)u−(a_0−2b_0)v\}\pmod N,
\quad Q_0=Q(a_0,b_0).
\]

When Q_0 is divisible by 3, the unit norm constraint Q(a,b)=q has no solutions. Otherwise it restricts (u,v) to an affine F_3-line with linear form L=((2a_0+b_0),−(a_0−2b_0)). The Weyl exponent is

\[
a r−2^{-1}ab=2^{-1}a(r+j)
\equiv 2^{-1}a_0(r+j_0)
+d\,2^{-1}\{(r+j_0)u+a_0v\}\pmod N.
\]

Thus its variable part is the nontrivial cubic character of M=((r+j_0),a_0). The determinant of the coefficient rows is

\[
\det(L,M)=2Q_0+2j_0(a_0−2b_0)\equiv2Q_0\not\equiv0\pmod3,
\]

because j_0 is divisible by 3. Hence M takes all three values on the affine line L=constant, and the three phases cancel by 1+ω+ω²=0. This proves F_q v_t=0. The E_s are sums of two unit-norm fibers q and −q, so they also annihilate v_t. Their supports {j_0,j_0+d,j_0+2d} are disjoint for distinct t in [0,N/9), establishing linear independence. QED.

## Theorem B (exact small-level kernel dimensions)

\[
\boxed{\dim K_9=1,\qquad \dim K_{27}=3,\qquad \dim K_{81}=9.}
\]

**Proof by reproducible exact modular rank certificate.** Stack the N×N matrices E_s vertically, giving an (N·N/3)×N matrix A_N with entries in Z[ζ_N]. Its kernel is K_N. At primes p≡1 mod N, choose a primitive N-th root u∈F_p and reduce ζ_N↦u. If the reduced matrix has rank r over F_p, then A_N has rank at least r over Q(ζ_N), because a nonzero r×r minor mod p lifts to a nonzero cyclotomic integer minor. The companion test performs modular Gaussian elimination on the full stacked matrix, yielding:

| N | p | ζ_N mapped to | exact F_p rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | 19 | 4 | 8 |
| 27 | 109 | 3 | 24 |
| 81 | 163 | 4 | 72 |

These reductions are valid: the chosen residues have exact orders N, and therefore satisfy the N-th cyclotomic polynomial mod p. Theorem A gives rank(A_N)<=N−N/9. The modular certificates give the reverse inequalities at N=9,27,81. Thus equality holds and the kernel dimensions are N/9 at those levels. QED.

## Corollary: exact common zero projectors at three levels

For N∈{9,27,81}, the common zero-eigenspace is precisely span{v_t:0<=t<N/9}. Its orthogonal projector is

\[
P_N=\frac13\sum_{t=0}^{N/9−1}|v_t\rangle\langle v_t|.
\]

This is the minimal joint zero-signature spectral projection of the commutative algebra generated by E_s, though its **operator rank** is N/9 (not one when N>=27). No assertion is made that the other joint signatures have a specified number or multiplicity at N=27,81.
