# All-depth proper-commutant obstruction from a growing common zero-signature class

**23 November 2026 — theorem.** Uses the exact all-depth common-kernel construction and the previously established MF-185 simple-spectrum result.

Let N=3^k, k>=1. Let U_T be the odd-modulus quantization of the Fibonacci cat map T=A², under the exact Egorov conventions of MF-185. Let D_N={U_T}' be its complex commutant. MF-185 proves U_T has simple spectrum and dim_C D_N=N. Let E_s be the primitive parity-even Weyl norm-square sums, and B_N=C*(I,{E_s:s∈(R^×)²})⊂D_N. These generators are commuting and self-adjoint.

## Theorem

For **every k>=1**, the generated algebra is a proper subalgebra:

\[
\boxed{B_{3^k}\subsetneq D_{3^k}.}
\]

For k>=2 its dimension obeys the exact upper bound

\[
\boxed{\dim_\mathbb C B_{3^k}\le N-\frac N9+1.}
\]

For k>=3 this is a strict upper bound on N. In particular dim B_27<=25, dim B_81<=73, and more generally the codimension in D_N is at least N/9−1.

## Proof

For k>=2, the preceding exact theorem WEYL_COMMON_KERNEL_GROWTH_AND_EXACT_RANK_CERTIFICATES_2026-11-23.md constructs N/9 independent vectors v_t=|3t>+|3t+N/3>+|3t+2N/3>, 0<=t<N/9, in the common kernel

\[
K_N=\bigcap_s\ker E_s,\qquad \dim K_N\ge N/9.
\]

Since E_s|_(K_N)=0 for every s, any unital polynomial in the generators acts as a scalar on K_N. The same holds for the entire finite-dimensional generated *-algebra B_N.

The generators commute with U_T by exact Egorov and orbit invariance. Since U_T has simple spectrum, there exists a joint orthonormal eigenbasis of U_T and all E_s. The subspace K_N is U_T-invariant and, because U_T is normal, it is spanned by exactly m=dim K_N distinct U_T eigenlines. All m eigenlines share the same joint E_s signature, namely the zero tuple.

A commutative *-subalgebra of D_N≅C^N is precisely an algebra of functions constant on blocks of a partition of the N eigenlines. One block has size at least m>=N/9. Thus the total number of blocks, which equals dim_C B_N, is at most N−m+1<=N−N/9+1. For k>=3, N/9>=3, so this is strictly less than N and B_N is proper.

At k=2, the earlier exact theorem WEYL_GENERATED_ALGEBRA_MOD9_EXACT_THEOREM_2026-11-23.md establishes dim B_9=5<9. At k=1, WEYL_GENERATED_ALGEBRA_SPECTRAL_CRITERION_2026-11-23.md establishes dim B_3=2<3. Hence properness holds for every k>=1. QED.

## Further consequences

1. **Quantitative codimension:** dim D_N−dim B_N>=N/9−1 for k>=2; the relative codimension is at least 1/9−1/N. This is a structural asymptotic obstruction.
2. **All-depth joint-signature collision:** for k>=3, at least N/9 cat-map eigenlines have exactly the same primitive parity-even Weyl signature, the zero tuple. No combination of these generators separates them.
3. **Extension to unit-norm generators:** the stronger annihilator theorem applies to all unit-norm fiber sums F_q. Therefore the unital algebra generated by all F_q (including parity-odd combinations), when embedded in the cat-map commutant via exact Egorov, also acts scalarly on K_N and is proper for k>=3. This extension is not claimed at k=1 or k=2.
4. **No all-depth kernel equality required:** the bound and properness use only dim K_N>=N/9, so the unproved conjecture dim K_N=N/9 for k>=5 is irrelevant to this theorem.

Depends on exact Weyl covariance for U_T, MF-185 simple spectrum, and the exact all-depth common-kernel lower bound. It does **not** require modular-rank certificates at N=27 or 81, does not compute the full generated algebra dimension for k>=3, and makes no physical or consciousness claim.
