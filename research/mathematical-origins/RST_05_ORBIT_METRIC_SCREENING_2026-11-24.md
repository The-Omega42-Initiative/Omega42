# RST-05 orbit metric and certified screening — 2026-11-24
For unit states x,y and compact unitary group G={e^(iθ)V^m}, define d=min_g ||x-gy||=sqrt(2-2 max_m |<x,V^m y>|). Compactness ensures attainment; symmetry by inversion, triangle inequality by composition and unitary invariance, identity on quotient by G. For orthogonal invariant projectors D_r, reverse triangle inequality yields sum_r (||D_r x||-||D_r y||)^2 <= ||x-gy||² for every g. Minimize to obtain layer lower bound. The same argument applies to spectral projectors, yielding a stronger bound because each layer is a sum of spectral projectors under simple spectrum, and reverse triangle inequality for the vector of spectral amplitudes within each layer.
