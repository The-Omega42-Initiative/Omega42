## Proof

Since 2r<=k, e²=N²/h² is divisible by N. Therefore the Weyl cocycle ζ_N^[±e²(a b'−b a')/2] is 1. The h² operators form an ordinary commuting group; their normalized sum is a self-adjoint idempotent. Equivalently, because W_N(ea,eb)=Z^(ea)X^(eb), its average factorizes as

\[
P_{k,r}=
\left(\frac1h\sum_{a=0}^{h-1}Z^{ea}\right)
\left(\frac1h\sum_{b=0}^{h-1}X^{eb}\right).
\]

The first factor projects onto basis indices j divisible by h, because ζ_N^(ej)=exp(2πij/h). The second factor averages the h-cycle j,j+e,...,j+(h−1)e. Since e divisible by h, these two projectors commute. Their joint fixed space has the stated normalized basis with N/h² disjoint supports. Hence rank P_(k,r)=N/h².

For r<s, the subgroup e_r(Z/h_r)² is contained in e_s(Z/h_s)²: e_r=3^(s−r)e_s, and h_s=3^(s−r)h_r. Thus the fixed space of the larger subgroup is contained in that of the smaller, and P_rP_s=P_s. For Egorov, an invertible symplectic T permutes each subgroup e(Z/h)², so conjugation preserves its group average. The difference-projector properties and ranks follow by nesting. The all-depth primitive annihilator theorem proves F_qP_(k,1)=0 and therefore F_qP_(k,r)=0 for r>=1; self-adjointness of E_s gives the two-sided statement.
