## The proposal

Existence has a midpoint or balance point, the Antipodes, where 5 − 4 = 1: the first distinction, the first domino to fall, the primordial fracture that gives rise to the perpetuation of existence.

## What is computed (CDT-A)

| | Integers, x = 1 to 8 | Modulo 9 |
|---|---|---|
| Antipode | 9 − x | −x; 0 is its own |
| Addition | 9 | 0 |
| Subtraction | 7, 5, 3, 1 (absolute) | 2x, one to one; only x = 5 gives 1 |
| Division | 1/8, 2/7, 1/2, 4/5: toward 1, never reaching it | −1 for every unit digit; undefined for 0, 3, 6 |

- Modulo 9, 1/2 = 5 and −1/2 = 4. The integer balance point 4.5 is no digit; modulo 9 it is the pair (−1/2, +1/2), one unit apart.
- T¹² = −I modulo 9: every nonzero state has a distinct Antipode half its period ahead (12 of 24, 4 of 8). On the canonical loop each digit and the digit twelve places on add to 9 or 0. The nine digits, an odd cycle, have no opposite point; the 24-step loop has one.
- Zero alone is fixed; zero with one distinction, (0, 1), generates an orbit, and the nine such seeds generate the family (Companion 39.6).

## Readings and hypotheses (CDT-C)

Four readings of the fracture: difference within unity; separation of opposites; symmetry breaking; self-distinction. The fracture as an ontological condition for succession rather than an event in a prior time. Balance preserved after differentiation.

## Points kept open or corrected

- "Deduction" is read as subtraction. If the Originator means logical inference, it is a separate operation with its own rules.
- GPT-6 notes that a reversible cycle has no privileged first state. True for each orbit; the record's privileged element is the pair of zero and one distinction, not a first state.
- GPT-6 notes that in a cycle of nine there is no opposite position. True of the digits; the Antipode of Omega42 is defined on the loop of states, where it is exactly opposite.
- Algebraic cancellation, (+x) + (−x) = 0, is not a physical conservation law.
- Open: is 1 only the smallest distinction, or the generator of the rest? It generates the integers modulo 9 and, as the seed (0, 1), an orbit.
